ESP32-C6 firmware rollback: hardware evidence, 2026-09-02
=========================================================

Node 3 (ESP32-C6, 16MB, CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_APP_ROLLBACK_ENABLE=y).
An image built to abort() 15 s after boot -- inside the 60 s
confirmation soak -- was pushed by OTA. The bootloader reverted it
and the node recovered on its own, with no USB intervention.
